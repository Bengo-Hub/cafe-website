**2\. ABOUT US**

Our Story

Urban Loft Café began in Kiambu in 2023, born from a dream to create spaces where people can eat well, connect meaningfully, work productively, and enjoy moments of calm. In 2024, we expanded to Busia.

Our Culture

We never compromise on food quality, service, hygiene, or customer comfort. We curate peaceful, premium experiences supported by mellow music and warm service.

Our Values

Excellence • Gratitude • Respect • Friendliness • Community • Experience

Our Vision

To become a leading East African lifestyle café brand built on community and premium experiences.

Our Tagline
